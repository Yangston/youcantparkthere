Review information for version 1.0.0 (9.1)

Guideline 2.3.8: The iOS container and Watch now use identical parking-sign artwork: black P, white background, green ring. Both targets explicitly include their AppIcon catalog.

1. Physical-device demonstration
youcantparkthere.mp4 is attached to the review notes and our October 1 message. It demonstrates the previous build 8.1; developer-reported device: Apple Watch Series 12, watchOS 27.0. This update changes icon packaging. Store screenshots are labelled simulator samples; sample hooks are disabled in release/device builds.

2. Purpose and audience
This free, independent Apple Watch app helps Bike Share Toronto riders find bikes or empty return docks. It shows availability, ordinary/e-bike counts when known, freshness and favourites. It does not find car parking, reserve/unlock bikes, process payments or record workouts. Availability may change before arrival.

3. Setup and features
Install the selected 1.0.0 build on an Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later and Internet access. The iOS bundle is a non-launchable watch-only distribution container; no iPhone app screen is expected. No account, credentials, subscription or paid content is required. No user-generated content or account-deletion flow exists.
On first launch select Browse downtown for real Toronto stations without GPS, or Enable location for nearby stations. Reviewers outside Toronto should choose Browse downtown; if GPS already centered elsewhere, pan to downtown Toronto. Release builds never fall back to demo stations.
Use Park/Bikes at bottom right for empty docks/total bikes. Tap a pin for docks, ordinary bikes, e-bikes, freshness and Favourite. White pin borders identify favourites. A dash means unknown, not zero. The center button recenters with GPS. Swipe horizontally across the bottom controls/page dots for Settings; drag above the strip to pan. Settings includes Refresh stations, GPS status and an offline privacy policy under Legal & data. Find Docks/Find Bikes complications are launchers without live counts.
Automatic cycling is optional and off by default. Enable it in Settings and allow Motion & Fitness. Confident unambiguous cycling for 12 seconds while active can start navigation and switch to Park. Background location is limited to detected cycling navigation. Walking/driving/running for 60 seconds or stationary evidence for 180 seconds stops it; disabling Automatic cycling stops it immediately. Navigation has a 90-minute limit and five-minute restart suppression after timeout/opt-out. A closed app cannot detect cycling. watchOS controls Smart Stack suggestions; relevance clears on stop/timeout/opt-out. No workout, HealthKit, silent audio or extended-runtime workaround is used.

4. External services/tools
Bike Share Toronto / Toronto Parking Authority public GBFS HTTPS feeds at toronto.publicbikesystem.net supply station information, status and vehicle-type metadata. Apple MapKit supplies maps, Core Location optional positioning, Core Motion opt-in activity classification, and WidgetKit/App Intents launchers/suggestions. No authentication/payment/AI service, ads, analytics SDK or developer backend is used. GPS/motion are not uploaded to the station feed or developer; preferences and public station caches are local.

5. Regional behaviour
Canada-only distribution; Bike Share Toronto-only coverage. The English interface and free features are the same throughout coverage. Outside Toronto, browse Toronto with Internet access.
