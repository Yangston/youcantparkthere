Native, watch-only app. The iOS bundle is Apple's non-launchable watch-only distribution container; there is no iPhone app screen and no sign-in is required. Please test on an Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later and Internet access.

Coverage is Toronto, Canada. Reviewers outside Toronto can choose Browse downtown on first launch (or deny location); this opens real Toronto station data. With GPS enabled elsewhere, pan the map to Toronto to inspect station availability. There is no sample-data fallback in release/device builds.

Use Park/Bikes at bottom right to switch counts. Tap a station for separate ordinary/electric counts and Favourite. The bottom controls and page dots also form a one-finger horizontal swipe surface for Settings; drag above them to pan the map.

Automatic cycling is off by default. Enable it in Settings and allow Motion & Fitness to test. Confident unambiguous cycling for 12 seconds while the app is active can begin navigation. Background location is used only for that automatically detected cycling navigation. Walking/driving/running for 60 seconds or stationary evidence for 180 seconds stops navigation. Turning Automatic cycling off stops it immediately. Navigation also stops after 90 minutes, with a five-minute restart pause after timeout or opt-out. No workout, silent audio or extended-runtime workaround is used. A closed app cannot detect cycling. Smart Stack suggestions are system-controlled and are cleared on stop/timeout/opt-out.

Privacy policy is available offline in Settings > Legal & data > Privacy policy. The app has no account, ads, analytics SDK or developer backend. It fetches public station data directly and does not upload GPS/motion to the operator feed. Public station inventory and favourites/preferences are cached locally.

The attached native simulator screenshots use explicitly labelled sample availability. They show production SwiftUI screens; demo hooks are Debug + simulator-only and cannot activate in the submitted binary. Actual availability comes from Bike Share Toronto and may differ. Apple MapKit attribution is retained.
