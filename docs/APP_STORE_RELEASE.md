## Submission checklist reference

1. **Decide whether your legal name is acceptable as the public developer/seller identity.** Individual memberships use the legal name; you do not create a separate developer nickname. Organizations can use a registered trade name, but Apple's developer-name choice is made when creating the first app and is not an ordinary editable field later. Your app record already exists. If you want a company identity, resolve enrollment/organization eligibility with Apple before publishing; do not delete the app or create replacement keys. Confirm the exact displayed legal name in Membership and App Store Connect. [Developer names](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/create-an-app-record/set-your-developer-name)
2. **Review account agreements and compliance yourself.** In App Store Connect > Business, resolve any pending developer agreements and account verification. Free apps do not require the Paid Apps Agreement just to be free; paid apps or in-app purchases require that agreement and tax/banking setup. Answer Apple's trader-status questions truthfully if prompted; Canada-only availability avoids an EU launch but does not justify inventing a status. [Workflow and agreements](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/get-started/app-store-connect-workflow), [DSA requirements](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-compliance-information/manage-european-union-digital-services-act-trader-requirements)
3. **Test the candidate on your physical Watch.** Install the new 1.0.0 beta and check map buttons, bottom swipes in both directions, favourites after relaunch, separate ordinary/e-bike counts, fresh/stale/closed states, location denied, network loss, repeated wake/recenter, complications, and automatic cycling stop/opt-out. Also check VoiceOver, large text, and battery use. Record hardware, OS, build and actual results in `docs/DEVICE_TESTS.md`. Simulator success is not proof of these device behaviours. Do not claim optional Accessibility Nutrition Label support until its requirements are physically verified.
4. **Review the public support/privacy pages.** Intended support URL: `https://yangston.github.io/youcantparkthere/`; privacy URL: `https://yangston.github.io/youcantparkthere/privacy.html`. Verify both load without sign-in and that the email works. `app-store/listing.json` contains proposed URLs, not proof of deployment. The manual **Publish support and privacy pages** workflow publishes only generated HTML/icon files and uses no Apple secrets. If Pages has not been enabled, set repository Settings > Pages > Source to GitHub Actions, then run that workflow on main. Keep the pages available for the lifetime of the app.
5. **Confirm privacy disclosures.** The source currently has no app account, ads, analytics SDK, developer backend, uploaded GPS/motion history, or tracking. Favourites/preferences and public station cache are local. Suggested App Privacy answer: **Data Not Collected**, provided you confirm the described practices remain true, including third-party code/services. Permission to use location/motion on-device is not itself collection. Apple Maps and the feed still receive network requests; support email/hosting have separate handling described in the policy. The bundled privacy manifest does not replace the App Store privacy questionnaire. Review and publish the accurate label yourself. [Apple privacy definitions](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/), [App Privacy setup](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/manage-app-privacy)
6. **Finish the editable App Store record.** Use Distribution > iOS App (the record also carries this watch-only app), set the version to **1.0.0** if it differs, select the processed **1.0.0** candidate build, and paste the prepared name/subtitle/description/keywords/promotional text. Suggested categories: Navigation, secondary Travel. Confirm copyright owner `2026 Stone Chuangyi Yang`. Set price **Free**, availability **Canada**, standard Apple EULA unless you intentionally provide your own, and **manual release**. There is no iPhone app UI to screenshot. Do not add fabricated iPhone screens.
7. **Upload and review screenshots.** Use `build/app-store/screenshots/en-CA/`: docks, bikes, favourites and station details. They are untouched native 422 x 514 Watch captures with explicitly labelled sample availability, not live inventory. Their source commit/device/runtime is in `provenance.json`; that provenance is for your records, not a product-page upload. Check the Media Manager's Apple Watch slot and use the same Watch size across localizations. Review imagery before submission and replace it with suitable native/device captures if you prefer live examples; do not paint over attribution or present fictional counts as live. [Watch screenshot sizes](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications)

## Important distinctions

- App name (editable marketing label), legal seller/developer identity, Team ID, numeric App Apple ID, bundle IDs and API Key ID are different. No new Apple key or app record is needed for public distribution. Signing credentials remain only in GitHub's protected `testflight` environment; see `APPLE_SIGNING.md`.
- TestFlight shows an iOS platform because Apple's non-launchable container delivers the Watch app. It does not mean there should be an iPhone app icon.
- Store availability in Canada does not expand service coverage beyond Bike Share Toronto. The description states this explicitly.
- No promise of closed-app cycling detection or a guaranteed clock banner belongs in screenshots or marketing.
- CI currently builds with Xcode 26.3. Recheck Apple's submission requirements on the actual submission date; Apple currently announces SDK 27 minimums beginning April 2027. [Submission requirements](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/submitting/)
- The privacy policy is a factual draft based on this repository and your supplied support address. Review it against your real support, hosting and data-handling practices before publishing the privacy label.
